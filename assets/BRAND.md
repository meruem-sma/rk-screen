İlk istem: “Production Windows app icon for RK Screen, a remote desktop and screen sharing app.
One original, balanced, compact geometric uppercase RK monogram with a subtle screen corner /
connection idea. Bold, legible at 16–48 pixels, optically centered, equal visual weight, sage
green #b2c6a7 inside a deep charcoal #18201d rounded-square tile; transparent outside the tile.
No extra words, mockups, watermarks, circuitry or imitation of AnyDesk/TeamViewer logos.”

Son düzeltme istemi: “Preserve the RK monogram shape, proportions, layout and rounded-square tile.
Make the sage monogram uniform #b2c6a7 and tile opaque #18201d. Remove grain, shading and accidental
transparent artifacts. Transparent pixels only outside the rounded-square boundary. Crisp small-icon edges.”
